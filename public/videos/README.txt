Hero background video ("drone view" of a city) — used by components/DroneBackground.tsx.

Files here (exact names matter):
  city-drone.webm   VP9, tried first by browsers that support it
  city-drone.mp4    H.264, works everywhere
  CREDITS.txt       source, creator, license and download date for the clip

To replace the video: keep the same file names. Recommended: 10-20 second
seamless loop, 1920x1080 or smaller, 24-30 fps, no audio, about 3-6 MB.
The site never shows the video on screens narrower than 640px, for visitors
who prefer reduced motion, or on Save-Data connections; they get the still
photo instead. Update CREDITS.txt if you change the clip.
